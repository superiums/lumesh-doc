Filter .log files larger than 1MB, get the top 5 by size

bash awk + sort + pipe concatenation 
ls -la *.log 2>/dev/null \
    | awk '$5 > 1048576 {print $5, $9}' \
    | sort -n | tail -5 | \
    | awk '{print $2, $1}'
VS
lumesh structured pipeline, complete in one go 
fs.ls -lah *.log ?. \
    | where(size > 1M) \
    | .sort('-size') \
    | .first(5)
