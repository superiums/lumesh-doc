## MATH Constants

| Constant | Meaning | Value |
|----------|---------|-------|
| E | Base of natural logarithm | 2.718281828459045 |
| PI | Pi (circumference of unit circle) | 3.141592653589793 |
| PHI | Golden ratio | 1.618033988749895 |

Usage:
```bash
math.sin(MATH.PI / 2)
```
